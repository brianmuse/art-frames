Art

A new painting every morning.

Search three museum collections and add a piece with one click. Or upload your own. Art Frames picks the next one each day, in order or shuffled.

  • Three museums. The Cleveland Museum of Art, the Met and the Art Institute of Chicago. Only public-domain images.
  • A mat for each piece. Fit or zoom, the focal point, and the mat's width and color.
  • Sent in 4K. Art Frames makes the full image, mat included, and sends it to the TV.

Try a mat.

With Fill the screen, the mat is the same width on all four sides. Art Frames crops the art to fit inside it.

Mat color

Up to 10% of the screen height. Set the width to zero for no mat. In the app, pick any color.

Room sensor · optional

On when you walk in. Dark when you leave.

A deep frame can cover the TV's own motion sensor. Add a small ESPHome presence sensor, and Art Frames runs the screen for you.

  • Hardware. Any ESPHome presence sensor, for example one with an LD2410 mmWave radar. Art Frames knows Apollo, Everything Presence, SCREEK, Seeed MR60 and Akamatis devices.
  • Local only. Art Frames finds the sensor on your network and reads it over the ESPHome native API.
  • How "off" works. In Art Mode, the Frame ignores the power key. Art Frames sets the TV's own Sleep After timer, and puts yours back when you stop.

Make your own

Your own layout, over the art.

A layout is a small website that sits over the day's painting like an overlay. Write it in plain HTML, or use a framework such as Astro. Art Frames turns the page into a 3840×2160 image and sends it to the TV at the times on your schedule.

  • It sees the art. Ask for the artwork, and each render gets the painting and its details. With a mat, it also gets the mat opening. Place your text where it reads well, and keep it inside the mat.
  • Settings for free. Declare settings in layout.json, and Art Frames makes the settings page. Private values, such as API keys, stay on your server.
  • Options for each TV. Add on/off options that each TV keeps for itself, such as "show the caption".
  • Server-side only. No JavaScript runs in the render. Bundle your fonts and images.

Install

Runs on a home server or a NAS.

Install it with Docker on any computer that stays on. It needs no cloud account. On a NAS, you can do every step in its Docker app.

  • A Samsung The Frame on the same home network.
  • A fixed IP address for the TV. Reserve it in your router.
  • Docker on a NAS or other always-on home server, 64-bit (amd64 or arm64). A Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 with a 64-bit OS should work too, but it is not on the compatibility list yet.
Read the install guide Compatibility 
# In an empty folder, such as art-frames
$ curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/brianmuse/\
art-frames/main/install/docker-compose.yml
$ docker compose up -d
$ docker compose logs art-frames
  1. Open the setup link from the log on your phone or computer.
  2. Create a password.
  3. Add your TV and pair it. Select Allow on the TV.
  4. Choose what it shows.

On Synology, Unraid or Portainer, paste docker-compose.yml into the Docker app instead. This is an early release: it needs tests on more TV models.

Or paste this to your agent

Your agent needs access to your server, over SSH or a terminal on it. You still create the password yourself.
