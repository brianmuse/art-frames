# Image credits

The README and the project website use these images. The website's `gallery-*.webp` thumbnails are small copies of the museums' open-access images. Every artwork and photograph in them is in the public domain. Each museum shares its image under Creative Commons Zero (CC0).

The screenshots come from Art Frames in demo mode (`art-frames --demo`), with no real TV, sensor or personal data. `crop-find-art.webp` and `crop-sensor.webp` are cropped from those screenshots. The TV images use made-up sample data: the names, events, headlines, news sources and weather are invented. The weather is a made-up forecast for Boston, MA.

## Find art results

- The Red Kerchief, Claude Monet, c. 1868–73
- Water Lilies (Agapanthus), Claude Monet, c. 1915–26
- Gardener's House at Antibes, Claude Monet, 1888
